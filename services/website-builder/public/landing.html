FAIR QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is the website really free?

The initial concept and presentation are free, with no obligation to buy. If you love it and choose to proceed, the website is $500 and hosting plus your live AI chat assistant is $50 a month. We confirm the scope before you commit.

Will you change my existing website?

Your concept is built separately. Your current site stays live while you review it. A launch or migration happens only after we agree on the work.

Can I ask for specific features?

Absolutely. Tell us what matters to you. Booking, ecommerce, payments, logins and other integrations may need additional work; we’ll make the scope clear during your walkthrough.

How does the AI assistant work?

It answers visitor questions using the information prepared for your website. Visitors can choose to hear responses aloud where their browser supports it. We review the content with you before launch.

Will this guarantee more leads or higher rankings?

No agency can promise that. We focus on a clearer, more useful website and sound technical foundations. Traffic and lead-generation services can be discussed separately.

What happens after I submit the form?

Your request goes to our team for qualification. We confirm the brief and a presentation time before a build begins. Submitting this form does not book an appointment automatically.
