FAQ

Fair questions

Is it really free on GitHub?

Yes — Rampcheck is MIT-licensed at github.com/bensblueprints/onboarding-checklist-mvp. The $29 buys the packaged Windows installer and updates; building from source is free.

Where does my onboarding content live?

In a SQLite file on your own server — your playbook stays your playbook, no cloud.

Is this a subscription in disguise?

No. $29 once, no renewal — still $29 in year three while Trainual is past $3,500.
