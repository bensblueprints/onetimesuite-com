FAQ

Fair questions

Is it really free on GitHub?

Yes, Slidecraft is MIT-licensed at github.com/bensblueprints/presentation-builder-mvp. The $29 buys the packaged Windows 1-click installer and updates; building from source is free.

Where do my decks live?

On your disk as plain-JSON .slidecraft files. Nothing is uploaded, no cloud, no telemetry.

Does it match Beautiful.ai feature for feature?

No, honestly. Beautiful.ai has more layout variety and AI design assistance. Slidecraft covers the seven layouts behind about 95% of real decks and does the same content-reflow trick offline.
